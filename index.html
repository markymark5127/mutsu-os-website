Mutsu OS

A Bazzite GNOME-derived, Mac-familiar Linux desktop with Steam/Proton gaming support, two visual styles (Calm Japandi and Cyberpunk), a compact Spotlight-style app/file launcher, and optional Mutsu AI help.

Calm Japandi

Sage wallpaper, light appearance, soft rounded UI, floating Dash-to-Dock, and a serene green accent. Designed for focused, beautiful daily computing.

Cyberpunk Edge

Harsh squared corners, neon gradients, high-contrast glitch aesthetics. Switch instantly when you want the full night-city energy.

Mac-Familiar

Window buttons on the left, 12-hour clock, hot corners off by default, Super+Space for Spotlight-style search. Fully overridable.

Gaming Ready

Built on Bazzite GNOME with full Steam/Proton hardware support intact. Play your library without compromise.

Mutsu Search

Native GNOME-integrated launcher with local app search, bounded file search, and optional AI fallback.

Alpha Status

Source and packaging exist. Bootable images and fresh-install verification are in progress. Test only on disposable VMs.
